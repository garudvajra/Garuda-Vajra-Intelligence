# GARUDA–VAJRA INTELLIGENCE

Independent Cyber Threat & Strategic Research Initiative

Focus areas: Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) · Cyber Threat Intelligence · Counter-Terrorism Research · Strategic Threat Analysis

We analyse verifiable public information to identify emerging threat patterns, document cyber activity, and publish evidence-based security assessments relevant to India and the wider region.

Principles: Evidence-led research, analytical integrity, privacy protection, lawful methods and responsible disclosure.

Independence: This is an independent research initiative and is not affiliated with or representing any government, military or intelligence agency.

Tagline: Observe. Analyse. Anticipate.
